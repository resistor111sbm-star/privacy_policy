Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our Expense & Finance Tracker application ("App") collects, uses, and safeguards your data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with expense tracking, income analytics, and report generation features, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Financial Data: Income, expenses, category details, and transactions that you manually enter into the App.
  • Exported Reports: Downloaded PDF or CSV files created from your tracking data.
  • Device Information: Basic device details (such as operating system version) for application stability and debugging.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Display your total balance, income, expense summaries, and trend analytics.
  • Generate downloadable financial reports in PDF and CSV formats.
  • Improve app usability, stability, and overall user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Data Protection: Your financial transaction records are stored securely to allow seamless access across your sessions.
  • Security Measures: We implement standard security practices to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal financial data to any third parties.
  • Data is only processed as necessary to fulfill essential app features or as required by applicable laws.

5. User Rights and Controls

  • Data Editing & Deletion: You can add, edit, or delete your transaction records at any time within the App.
  • Exporting Data: You can export your financial data into CSV or PDF format whenever needed.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our support channels.
